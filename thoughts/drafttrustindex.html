Low- and High-Trust Societies

I dream of the day I can leave my bike unlocked on the back of my car while I run into the store. I dream of the day I can leave my laptop unattended in the coffee shop while I go to the restroom. I dream of the day kids can be told "just be home before dark" again, like I was.

I dream of these because I live in the fairly low-trust society of the United States. Of course, there exist enclaves of different levels of trust, e.g., Memphis is very low trust and Highland Park is very high trust, but I'd argue that most of the U.S. is on the lower end (or I'm just projecting and/or bringing my baggage with me when visiting other cities).

The U.S. Joint Economic Committee Republicans actually authored a wonderful report called the The Geography of Social Capital in America (interactive map here), that discusses what they refer to as the "social capital index" as defined in the report.
