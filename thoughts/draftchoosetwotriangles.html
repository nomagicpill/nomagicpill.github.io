Choose-Two Triangles Are Everywhere, But Cannot Be Defeated

Published

Contents

A choose-two triangle (C2T) happens when there are three variables present, but only two can be selected at the expense of the third. Below are a few examples with all combinations. Note that while they may not be perfect and there are definitely some exceptions, all of them are at least conceivable.

Manufacturing: Cheap, Fast, High-Quality

Cheap + fast = poor quality

Cheap + high quality = slow

Fast + high quality = expensive

Food: Cheap, Healthy, Delicious

Cheap + healthy = tastes bad

Cheap + delicious = unhealthy

Healthy + delicious = expensive

Personal Life: Career, Health, Social

Career + health = no social life

Career + social life = no health

Health + social life = no career

(This was originally for college, but feels like it can easily extend to post-college situations.)

But I Want All Three!

Why is it that we can only choose two? Shouldn't it be possible to choose all three?
