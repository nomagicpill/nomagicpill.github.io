McDonald's

In 2015, McDonald's announced all-day breakfast. This was a genius idea in my (uneducated, naive, ignorant) opinion: everyone loves breakfast. Everyone gets hungry later in the day. This was a no-brainer decision by McDonald's!

I proudly announced my intent to buy some McDonald's shares quickly because the stock price was sure to go up. My dad looked at me and responded something along the lines of:

No Magic Pill, you won't make any money. There a legions of Stanford and MIT PhDs out there who knew this was coming and already priced it in.

Now I'm not sure if my dad knew the efficient-market hypothesis by name, but he sure knew the concept.
