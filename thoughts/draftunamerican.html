Consumer Nullification

Capitalism is built upon competition that ultimately drives improvements in all categories, from price reductions to quality to safety. Slowly but surely everything gets better as consumers jump ship from product A to product B after a change, then back to product A after company A makes a reactive change to compete with company B.

This race to being the best is effectively nullified when consumers choose to blindly buy based on something other than the overall attractiveness of the product; this is the capitalistic version of jury nullification. They don't care about the product being good, they just care about the cause of #BuyAmerican or #SupportAVeteran or whatever else is causing product improvement to stagnate.

Why Blind Buyers Are Wrong

The best product should be rewarded by being chosen. Period. Choosing based on any characteristic other than "best" is disrespectful to whoever is being favored because it implies they aren't capable of actually competing with the best. This is the capitalistic version of affirmative action, where businesses who are X-owned get by with subpar products simply because they have the X-owned branding.
