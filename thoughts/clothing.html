Theory

Clothing follows its own hierachy of needs not entirely unlike Maslow's famous pyramid. In its base form, it's meant to protect the wearer if the environment isn't already doing so—from the elements, from impropriety, and so on. Its purpose after that is up for debate, but can include helping to gain and maintain membership to a group, maintaining culture and customs, expressing opinions, improving physical appearance.

With this in mind, clothes should be carefully chosen for a specific purpose.

Purposes

The following purposes are "exercises" (of sorts) that will allow for personal development through selective choice of clothing.

Improving Conversation

Wearing as plain of clothes as possible reduces, if not eliminates, the risk of others commenting on the outfit. The plain outfit also suggests the wearer is boring.

Both of these reasons force the wearer to find other things to talk about with the observer, improving their conversation skills.

Potential Situations

  • Making small talk in public with strangers
  • Dating

Making Connections

Wearing clothes that have a coded message can result in:

  • Others who are ignorant about the content asking about it, creating the potential for a connection
  • Others who are aware of the content asking about it, immediately forming a connection
