The Average Best Day Ever

Most people's best days ever revolve around some major life event: their engagement, their wedding, their child being born, their company being acquired, winning some major award. This is great! Those people should be happy because those are amazing experiences.

But what if you could manufacture a best day ever by building it from the ground up, by designing every part of the day down to the finest details to maximize happiness. Imagine how big the area under the happiness curve would be!
