Background

There are a bunch of things that exist that I think everyone should try at least once. Sometimes people can't afford (read: choose not to afford) these things. I think they are wrong in choosing not afford these things and this is my way of encouraging that they do said things.

Below is a running list of things (along with the amount of money I'm willing to give) that I will subsidize with some basic requirements:

  1. For things that can be obtained in "other ways", I ask that you attempt those ways first if you are morally willing.
  2. You send me receipt of the item. This is preferably a picture of the item itself in a very not-a-stock-photo way or literal receipt.

Requirement 1 is the honor system, requirement 2 is required since it is such a low cost to prove.
