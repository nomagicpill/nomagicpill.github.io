Background

On 28 March 2025 I injured my right elbow. I suspect it was from trying to put a bicycle tire on a wheel without tire levers—lame, I know. 10 weeks later at the time of this writing it's still not fully healed. I lack full extension and flexion.

This post, while relating directly to Notes II: Medicine, is a standalone for a few reasons:

  • It's long and detailed (I guess I could've put it in a collapsible section, but it feels better here)
  • I document personal diagnoses and results, which feels wrong to put in a notes page
  • I want to learn about the elbow in great detail and this affords me the opportunity as opposed to just using one of the LLM research tools

Structure

Before effusion are discussed, it's helpful to understand how the elbow is structured.

Merck Manuals has a nice 3D model of the elbow bones:

  • Humerus: The bone of the upper arm. The capitellum (also spelled capitulum) is on the outside and interfaces with the radius. The trochlea is on the inside and interfaces with the ulna.

Aspiration

Synovial fluid can be aspirated, or removed, from the elbow joint via a large needle.
