Bounties

I offer what I consider generous bounties for information or material related to each trail. Expand the trail sections to see the conditions and payout. Please contact me with the bounty requirements clearly labeled in order to collect.

I will not release any bounty information or material to the general public unless the information provider explicitly says I can.

Background

In 6th grade I discovered the North Shore, Vancouver's insane mountain biking area comprising Mt. Fromme, Mt. Seymour, and Cypress Mountain. For whatever reason, Fromme was what intrigued me the most and what has stuck with me to this day.

There are few resources out there that compile all the materials on a single trail in a single place. Some of it's on Pinkbike, some on YouTube, some on Facebook, some in people's garages on hard drives gathering dust. This is my attempt to gather as many materials as possible in a single place for reference and posterity.

If you have more materials that aren't shown here but you think should be, please contact me.

Disconstructed Wheel

Bounty information:

  • Payout: $100-1000
  • Requirements: An exact latitude and longitude of the location of the Wheel, even if it's not still standing. Proof in the form of a video is required and can be verified using the videos.
  • Notes: As far as I can tell, the Wheel wasn't built specifically for NWD—it appears Sam built it before the film. That being said, there's almost literally nothing on the internet that I can find on it. Pictures are sparse, there's one video, and only rumors of its location. I even reached out the Sam's sister, but never heard back.
