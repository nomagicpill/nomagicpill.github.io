Background

I find it super cool when people find ingenious ways to get things done, so I wanted to capture all of them I find in a single place.

Assume all stories are true. I'll do some research to verify veracity, but not a lot.

List

  • Israel created a shell company to manufacture and sell explosive-laden pagers to their enemies. Apparently the company was even profitable.
  • A former U.S. Special Forces soldier and his son smuggled a house-arrested CEO out of Japan in a music box.
  • I can't find this tweet anymore, but some startup founder really wanted a person's attention, so they purchased ad space on every billboard on said guy's way to work in an effort to coax him into helping them.
