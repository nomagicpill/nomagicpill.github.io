The Good Ol' Days

I consider 2012-2015 to be the golden age, the heydey, the good ol' days of street workout. There was a beautiful simplicity in the scene at that time. Teenagers, mostly Slavic, were achieving world-class levels of strength using the most basic of playground equipment and in a variety of environments. I saw videos of seemingly everyday guys doing skills in their bedroom that gymnasts spend years training (there's a specialization factor here, of course, but the point still stands), while others were training on playgrounds between giant Soviet bloc-style apartment buildings. It was amazing. It was a testament to their ambition and hard work, a direct insult and contradiction to those who claim they need chalk and perfect knurling and humidity control and supplements galore and insane sleep. These guys were blowing past the limits that Big Fitness had put on them.
