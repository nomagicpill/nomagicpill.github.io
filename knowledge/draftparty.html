Theory

Parties are fun. What else needs to be said?

Parties should have a purpose, first and foremost. Having fun is probably the first and foremost goal of most, if not all, parties. As it should be! Who (and why) would someone go to a party that's not fun?

It's the secondary or tertiary reasons that decide the theme and logistics.

The first paragraph is the hook that can be copy-pasted into the party description. Everything after that is just supporting info.

This is your chance to live out your wildest (or meanest) dream: dressing like [name of the host(s)]! [FINISH]

Guests should dress like the host(s) normally dress(es). If a couple is hosting, then women dress like the woman and men dress like the man. For example, if Steve Jobs were hosting, guests would come dressed in a black turtleneck and blue jeans.

For a fun, weird twist, women can dress like the man and vice versa, or if it's a single person hosting, imagine how they would dress if they were the other gender.

Stop what you're doing right now. Yeah, you. Look down at what you're wearing. Take a picture. Write it in a note. Figure out a way to remember, because this is exactly what you should be wearing to this party. It's the honor system, so we can't really tell if you change, but the honor system is what makes the world go around and you really don't wanna erode public trust more than it already is, right? Right! So we'll see you and that outfit soon!

Upon opening the invitation for the first time, guests should take note of exactly what they're wearing. Exactly. I don't have a good answer for if they're just in their underwear or similar, but that's probably something to take up with the host.
