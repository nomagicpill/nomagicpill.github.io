Jet lag plan for long international flights, from Lincoln Quirk. Melatonin and light drag the body clock onto destination time; quitting caffeine before the flight piles up enough sleepiness to actually sleep on the plane. The caffeine half only works with a habit to quit (~1-2 cups of coffee a day).

Planning






Example: New York to London is +5 hours. With a 10:30pm bedtime, that's 5:30pm in New York, so the melatonin alarm is 5:00pm New York time. On a 6:00pm departure that lands at 6:00am London time, melatonin happens at the gate and the whole flight is London night.

Lincoln Quirk's Jetlag Cures

Jetlag Cures, turned into the Long-Haul Flight Checklist above. The model: sleepiness is a body clock (set by melatonin, nudged by daylight) plus sleep pressure (adenosine, which caffeine blocks). Melatonin and light move the clock to the destination; withdrawing from caffeine lets the pressure a coffee habit builds up hit all at once, right when it's time to sleep on the plane. His own caveat:

not particularly supported by science, works on my machine, your results may vary.
