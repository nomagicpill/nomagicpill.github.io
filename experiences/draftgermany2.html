On Vibes

Berlin is a dreary city. What it lacks in industry it makes up for in its counter-cultural scene and deep history of the 1900s. I can feel the punk-ish-ness radiating off of the seemingly young population. They’re dressed well, not in a suit-tie manner, but a this-will-get-me-into-Berghain manner. The rate of people with colorful hair and unique haircuts and visible piercings is much higher than any other place I’ve ever been. They have their priorities in order: enjoy life, then work. (The city’s housing rent cap makes this much easier for those with jobs that don’t pay high wages.)

[Berlin doesn't feel very, uh, industrious. It feels artsy and punky and counterculture. But it doesn't necessarily feel like people are working very hard here in the same way that it does. San Francisco or New York or Chicago. Went to the Stasi Museum, and it's amazing how oppressive they were and totalitarian. And just all encompassing in everyday life. I wonder if that led to some sort of... boomerang effect, but rubber band of sorts, where... they were so oppressive that it artificially selected for... ever, A, people who were fine being oppressed, and the counter revolutionary died, or, B, more evidently. people who were counterculture and said, I said, FU to the system and all of that.]

Munich feels different. The people here walk with a purpose; they have somewhere to be and something to do. It’s cleaner, giving a different character than graffiti- and litter-covered Berlin. The parks are wonderful. The grass and trees are green and lush and no music is being played out loud and the birds are obviously well-fed given one of them shit on me. The suburbs are calm and small. Nobody appears to be anxious about anything.

Social Barbells

We invented a new application of an old concept: social barbells. We spoke nonstop the entire time we were together, while I was almost completely quiet other times. Is this the way? Does the need for socialization build up inside you over time to the point that it’s that much better when it comes time to hang? Maybe.
