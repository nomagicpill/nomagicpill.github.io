Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

This policy covers the personal apps and tools made by nomagicpill ("I," "me") that let users sign in with Google or connect a Google account (each an "app"). These are small personal projects, not commercial products.

Contents

Information I Collect

When users sign in with Google, the app receives only the information they approve on Google's consent screen. Usually this is their basic profile: name, email address, and profile picture.

If an app asks for access to other Google data (for example, a calendar or files), the consent screen lists exactly what it asks for, and the app uses only that.

The apps don't collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or location data.

How I Use It

Users' information is used only to sign them in and to provide the app's features to them. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to build profiles of users.

Google User Data

The apps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Google user data is:

  • used only to provide or improve features users can see in the app;
  • not transferred to anyone else, except as needed to provide those features, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale with the user's consent;
  • not used for advertising, and not sold;
  • not read by a person unless the user gives permission, it is needed for security or to comply with the law, or it has been aggregated and anonymized.

Storage and Security

Data is stored only as long as the app needs it to work, using the security protections of the services the app runs on. Access tokens are kept private and are never shared. No system is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable care to protect users' information.

Sharing

I don't sell, rent, or trade users' information. I share it only when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

User Choices and Deletion

Users can remove an app's access to their Google account at any time from their Google Account permissions page. To have any data an app has stored about them deleted, users can email me (see Contact), and I will delete it within 30 days.

Children

The apps aren't intended for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
