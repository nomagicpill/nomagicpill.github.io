On loss of power, a valve settles into whichever state it's built for — there's no in-between.

  • N.O. — Normally Open: powered = closed, power lost = open. Used where the gas should keep flowing through a fault.
  • N.C. — Normally Closed: powered = open, power lost = closed. Used where the gas must stop flowing through a fault.
