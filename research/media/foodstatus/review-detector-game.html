What is a Brier Score?
The Brier score measures the accuracy of your probabilistic predictions. It ranges from 0 (perfect predictions) to 1 (worst possible predictions). Random guessing would give a score of 0.25.

How it's calculated:
For each question, we calculate (confidence - actual)², where confidence is your predicted probability (as a decimal) and actual is 1 if you were correct or 0 if incorrect. The final score is the average across all questions.
