Background

From my apartment living room I can see two distinct storefront signs (the ones on the outside of a business used for locating and advertising), one for a grocery store and one for a diner. The grocery store can be seen from the nearby busy intersection and highway while the diner is tucked away and less visible, but both have their signs on 24/7. Even at midnight and 5:00am the lights are on. They never go off. Ever. (At least as far as I've been awake at those odd hours.)

How is this economical? Is it really worth it in electricity costs to keep them on in the very early morning? Or is the business owner just a buffoon?

Cost

According to this totally reputable website, LED neon lights use 9 W per meter per hour, giving us a total cost as follows assuming the period in question is 10:00pm to 6:00am where m is total length in meters of the sign:

cost = 9 W/m-hr × 8 hour × meters = 72m kWh

Benefit

The benefit is exclusively in advertising to passerby, both in introducing potential newcomers and reminding current patrons of their existence.
