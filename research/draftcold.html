Published

Preparing for cold water torture is like preparing to get kicked in the balls. There's no preparing for it and it sucks every time.

Contents

Motivation

Improving my tolerance to cold weather is good for my life. I wouldn't have to wear or pack as much for cold weather days or trips. Cold showers are generally considered to be healthier than warm showers. I'd have more access to places on Earth with less required resources. The list goes on.

I am not deluding myself into thinking I'll be summitting Everest or trekking across Antarctica in shorts and flip-flops. But if I can be 10% more comfortable at low temperatures, I'll gladly take it—that's free hedons!
