A Week in the Life Of

Here are a few example weeks that show what an engineer could experience. These are fictional, but not out of the realm of possibilities.

Week 1: A Little Bit of Everything

Monday

Passdown at 7:00am. The night shift technicians tell us dayshifters what happened to the equipment last night and the current state of it. I ask questions if I need to. We go over other important thing: upcoming maintenance, metrics (availability, fail modes and counts, mean time between failure), who's out of the office over the next few days. The engineers then meet to discuss their rough plans for the day, whether it's working on projects or helping to recover their equipment so it can get to producing chips again.

After passdown, I go through the weekend emails, marking some as respond-to-later or taking care of them as I read them.
